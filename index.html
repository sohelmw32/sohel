03 / A quick explanation

Land first.
Build in stages.

With many packages, the land settles first. The construction loan is then drawn in stages as the build progresses.

During construction, interest is generally charged on the funds drawn. The repayment type, rate and payment process depend on the lender and your loan.

“Turnkey” describes the promised finished home. Check the contracts and inclusions to understand what you’re buying and when you need to pay.

HOW THE FUNDING CAN FLOW
  1. 01
    Land settlementYour land funding and contribution are paid.
  2. 02
    Construction progressesPayments follow the agreed schedule and lender checks.
  3. 03
    Build completeThe loan moves to its agreed ongoing repayment arrangement.

04 / First-home buyer planning

A smaller deposit.
Dates to keep in view.

Eligible buyers may use the Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme to build with a minimum 5% deposit and no LMI. Other costs, price caps and lender requirements still matter.

The timeline below applies to eligible Scheme builds. Your lender may set additional or shorter deadlines.

Have both the land contract and eligible building contract in place before home-loan settlement.

  1. 12monthsStart building

    Within 12 months of home-loan settlement.

  2. 36monthsFinish building

    Within 36 months of home-loan settlement.

  3. 6monthsMove in

    Within 6 months of the occupancy certificate being issued.

Already owned land before securing a Scheme place? That construction loan isn’t eligible under this pathway. We can discuss other lending options.

06 / Before you ask

Good questions.
Plain answers.

Here are a few that come up when clients are planning a build.

Ask me about your situation ↗
Is the builder’s deposit all the cash I need?+

No. A contract deposit is a payment to the seller or builder. Your overall contribution also depends on the lender’s valuation, approved loan, purchase costs and any unfunded extras. We’ll work through when your own funds are needed.

Can I use equity in land I already own?+

Potentially. We need a current lender valuation, the existing loan balance and an assessment of what you can repay. Equity may reduce the cash you need to contribute, but it doesn’t automatically increase borrowing capacity.

Does a fixed-price contract cover every extra?+

Check the inclusions, exclusions and allowances. Variations, site conditions and your own selections may add costs. Have your solicitor or conveyancer review the contract, and tell your broker about changes before assuming the bank will fund them. Extra lending may require reassessment.

Will I pay the full loan repayment from day one?+

Construction interest is generally based on the amount drawn, with interest-only payments often used during the build. Your land loan may have its own repayments. We’ll check the actual loan terms and allow for both, alongside your rent and other commitments.

Does a 10% deposit mean I avoid LMI?+

Not automatically. LMI commonly applies when borrowing more than 80% of the lender-assessed property value, unless an eligible scheme, waiver or other arrangement applies. Your total contribution also needs to cover costs outside the loan.

Are grants or stamp duty savings available?+

Some eligible NSW buyers can access the First Home Owner Grant or transfer-duty assistance. They are separate from the federal deposit guarantee and have their own property, residency and other conditions. We can identify what to check; your conveyancer can confirm the duty position.

Check Revenue NSW ↗
Is pre-approval enough to sign a building contract?+

Pre-approval is conditional. The lender still needs to assess the proposed property, building documents and any outstanding conditions. Confirm the finance position and obtain legal advice before making a binding commitment.
